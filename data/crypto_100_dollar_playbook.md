## ⚠️ The $100 Golden Rules (Capital Preservation Math)

1. **Avoid Ethereum Mainnet (L1) Gas Fees:** A single Uniswap swap on Ethereum L1 can cost \$5 to \$30 in gas. Two transactions will destroy 10% to 60% of a \$100 portfolio.
2. **Chain Hierarchy for \$100:**
   - **Tier 1 (Sub-cent fees):** **Solana** (~$0.0005/tx), **Base** (~$0.005/tx), **Arbitrum** (~$0.02/tx), **Sui** (~$0.001/tx), **Polygon** (~$0.01/tx).
3. **Execution Realism:** Do not buy 10 different assets with \$100 ($10 each) on high-fee chains. Keep positions concentrated in 1 to 3 assets at a time.
